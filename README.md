# B2PRIME IB Commission Calculator

A partner tool for Introducing Brokers to estimate their commission earnings across B2PRIME's trading platforms, account types, and asset classes.

## What it does

The calculator lets partners select a trading platform (cTrader or B2TRADER), account type, and asset group to instantly see estimated IB earnings based on:

- **Partnership tier** — Core, Prestige, Elite, or PRIME
- **Trading volume** — adjustable via slider or direct input
- **Symbol** — real commission rates per asset
- **Network levels** — earnings broken down across all 4 referral levels (direct clients + sub-IB chain)

## Platforms & Account Types

| Platform | Account Types |
|----------|---------------|
| cTrader | Raw (Commission Plan), Standard (Lot Plan) |
| B2TRADER | Raw, Unified, SPOT Crypto, Perpetual Futures |

## IB Tier Structure

| Tier | Revenue Share |
|------|---------------|
| Core | 60% of max rate |
| Prestige | 70% of max rate |
| Elite | 80% of max rate |
| PRIME | 100% of max rate |

Tier eligibility is based on cumulative trading volume and active client count over the last 3 months.
